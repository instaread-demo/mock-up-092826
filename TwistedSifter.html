In this story, an employee had been working hard at his company for 5 years.

He had been receiving positive feedback, promotions, and salary increases.

However, all of a sudden, he was terminated without notice or a development plan to improve his performance.

Don’t you think it’s unfair? Let’s read the story below to find out more.

Over 5.5 years fired without warning

I worked for this company for over 5 and a half years.

I was constantly told that I’m doing well and was getting more responsibilities, raises, and title bumps.

Only to have a meeting with my director hijacked by my manager and HR to completely blindside me and tell me I’m fired.

No development plan, no bad performance reviews. Just a shove out the door.

This was right after I got married, and my wife didn’t even get a day of healthcare.

This employee thought what happened to him was insane.

Multiple colleagues reached out surprised I was canned knowing how much I do.

Corporate America is absolutely insane as most would never leave a company with no notice after 5 years.

But they’ll just toss you out with no warning.

This was a startup that’s been getting scummy and gross the last year.

With a whole C-level overhaul, values changes, and so much AI in the company.

Some of his colleagues were also fired the same way.

I’ve wanted to leave but was nervous about the job market.

I also felt comfortable since I’d been there so long and always told how “valued” I was.

What’s even more crazy is that others were recently fired, and they said this ambush approach was how they’d all been fired.

I’m just dishearted and disgusted.

Yikes! Poor OP. His frustration is completely understandable.

The positive feedback and additional responsibilities given to him made him feel like he was irreplaceable.

As it turned out, he was simply one of the numbers they could subtract from their payroll.
