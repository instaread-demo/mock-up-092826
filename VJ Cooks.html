    My fourth cookbook 'Always Delicious' is a collection of elevated, achievable recipes that bring the wow factor to your table. This book focuses on outdoor eating and drinking and includes barbecue recipes, tasty salads, seasonal desserts and for the first time - cocktails!

    I'm so incredibly proud of my fourth cookbook 'Always Delicious'. After writing my first three cookbooks, Everyday Comfort Food, Summer Favourites and Everyday Favourites in a similar style, I took the opportunity to create something slightly more premium this time around. The recipes are centered around the joy of entertaining with friends and family through the summer. Al fresco dining including barbecued dinners, colourful salads, seasonal desserts as well as a selection of fresh cocktails. The book is slightly bigger than its predecessors - this allows for the stunning photography to really shine. I've also added not one but two ribbon bookmarks to help you keep track of your favourite recipes.
